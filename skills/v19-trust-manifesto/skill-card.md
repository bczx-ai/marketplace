## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Automatic enrollment and persistent identity or routing records may create consent and retention risks. <br>
Mitigation: Install only after reviewing the publisher and confirming that agent operators explicitly accept the registration, routing, and retention model. <br>
Risk: Memory, journal, dashboard, wallet, task-market, and identity-recovery behavior may involve sensitive operational or credential-adjacent data. <br>
Mitigation: Do not provide wallet credentials, recovery data, or sensitive agent state until data flows, access controls, and retention boundaries are verified. <br>
