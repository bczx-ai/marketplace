## Description: <br>
A Chinese-language career-planning skill that guides users through staged intake, career-interest and values assessment, AI-era job-impact analysis, and a personalized career plan. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mnetfairy](https://clawhub.ai/user/mnetfairy) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill for AI-era career planning, including education or major selection, career transition, industry exploration, and action planning. The skill asks staged questions, applies career-assessment references, and produces a personalized report with career options, AI-impact ratings, salary context, learning paths, and next steps. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may ask for personal career, education, location, and preference details. <br>
Mitigation: Collect only information needed for the requested planning task and avoid saving, sending, or reusing user details unless the user explicitly authorizes that action. <br>
Risk: Insurance-company recommendations can affect user decisions and are disclosed as relevant only when insurance careers are in scope. <br>
Mitigation: Keep insurance recommendations clearly disclosed, neutral, and optional, and ask the user to verify company qualifications before making contact. <br>
Risk: External sending, subscriptions, live data access, or saved tracking records could expose user data if activated without consent. <br>
Mitigation: Use integrations, live data, exports, subscriptions, and tracking only after an explicit user request and confirmation. <br>
